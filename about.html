Three Students. One Country to Explore.

We are Ramazan Utegen, Ziyadinkhan, and Ruzimuhammad: three students from SE-2501 sharing a guide to four cities in Kazakhstan. Built with HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap, our project brings together places we want you to discover.

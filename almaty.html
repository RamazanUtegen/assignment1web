About Almaty Region

Almaty is surrounded by the stunning Trans-Ili Alatau mountains. Here are the top locations you must visit:

Must-See Natural Spots

  • Big Almaty Lake: High-altitude turquoise alpine reservoir.
  • Charyn Canyon: Dramatic rock formations resembling the Grand Canyon.
  • Kolsai Lakes: A system of picturesque mountain lakes.

Cultural & Historical Sites

  • Medeu Skating Rink: The highest Olympic-sized ice rink in the world.
  • Shymbulak Ski Resort: Premier ski resort in the Zailiysky Alatau.
  • Kok Tobe Park: Scenic hilltop park with panoramic city views.

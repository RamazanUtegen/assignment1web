# KZ Travel full visual redesign

**Request:** Completely redesign the existing site to feel modern and pleasant. The user selected a calm, modern and light direction.

**Design:** `DESIGN.md`; product and preservation constraints: `PRODUCT.md`.

Replace the previous condensed blue/lime editorial identity with DM Sans, a Caspian teal accent, cool white surfaces, rounded photographic framing and comfortable spacing. The first viewport pairs clear copy with a large landscape; mobile stacks the copy before the photo. Existing discovery and route-planning interactions remain the primary actions.

The alternatives were an immersive photo background and a restrained split composition. The latter keeps the user's selected light mood and makes copy readable across all existing photographs. The reference study's uneven photographic placement informs the opening; equal destination columns and three-column gallery exercises remain to satisfy the project requirements.

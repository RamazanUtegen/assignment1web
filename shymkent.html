About Turkistan Region

Shymkent is one of Kazakhstan's most vibrant cultural centers. Here are the top locations you must visit:

Must-See Natural Spots

  • Aksu-Zhabagly Nature Reserve: Central Asia's oldest reserve.
  • Sayram-Ugam National Park: Scenic waterfalls and mountain trails.
  • Kyzylkol Lake: Healing mud and mineral springs.

Cultural & Historical Sites

  • Astanaly Mausoleum (Turkistan): UNESCO World Heritage site nearby.
  • Shymkent Dendropark: Massive green park with rare plant species.
  • Old City Bazaar: Traditional bustling oriental market.

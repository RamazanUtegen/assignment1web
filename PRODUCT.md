# KZ Travel

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

An educational six-page Kazakhstan travel guide made by the SE-2501 team. Visitors explore Aktau, Astana, Almaty and Shymkent, save cities and build a short inspiration route.

## Operating Context

Static HTML, local Bootstrap, local fonts and vanilla JavaScript. It works on a local server and directly from files. No account, backend, real bookings or form submissions. Existing assignment requirements and team credits must remain.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Combined destination search/category filters; saved places; three-step validated planner; nine-slide galleries with thumbnails; FAQs; demonstration forms and contact dialog; local themes and ratings; optional Web Audio chime; live Kazakhstan clock. Keep original photo files and fallback paths. Preserve keyboard, reduced-motion and unavailable-storage behavior.

## Brand Commitments

Name: KZ Travel. On 9 November 2026, the user requested a complete modern, pleasant redesign and selected a calm, modern and light direction. English site content and the four city guides remain the product's existing content.

## Evidence on Hand

Original Kazakhstan photos in `images/`; responsive derivatives in `assets/optimized/`; assignment documentation and regression scripts in `docs/` and `scripts/`. No verified customer reviews, booking inventory or commercial claims are supplied.

## Product Principles

- Let visitors compare real destinations and reach a city guide quickly.
- Keep the website usable without a service or account.
- Preserve the educational demonstrations and credit their authors.
- Keep all interactions accessible with mouse, touch and keyboard.
